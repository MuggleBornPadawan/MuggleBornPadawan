# Research Report: TIME100 Art (2026)

- **Source URL**: https://time.com/collection/time100-art/2026/
- **Published Date**: September 17, 2026
- **Project**: Inaugural TIME100 Art list
- **Publisher**: TIME Magazine

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- TIME published its first dedicated **TIME100 Art** list in September 2026.
- The list recognizes 100 influential figures in the global visual art ecosystem.
- The list has 97 entries. Three entries contain pairs of people (97 + 3 = 100 individuals).
- The entries divide into four categories of ~25 entries each:
  - **Leaders** (25 entries)
  - **Titans** (23 entries, 24 people)
  - **Icons** (24 entries, 25 people)
  - **Innovators** (25 entries, 26 people)

---

## 2. Lead Profiles (Cover Stories)

Each category features one primary long-form profile:

1. **Leaders Lead**: **Amy Sherald**
   - *Article*: "Amy Sherald Would Rather Walk Away Than Water Down Her Art"
   - *Descriptor*: "People painter"
   - *Author*: Belinda Luscombe
   - *Focus*: Known for her portrait of Michelle Obama. She refuses commercial pressure to compromise her artistic vision.

2. **Titans Lead**: **Alicia Keys and Swizz Beatz (Kasseem Dean)**
   - *Article*: "Alicia Keys and Swizz Beatz Turned Collecting Art Into a Mission"
   - *Descriptor*: "Thinking big"
   - *Author*: Erin McMullen
   - *Focus*: Major collectors of Black artists. Organized the *Giants* exhibition at the Brooklyn Museum.

3. **Icons Lead**: **Diya Vij**
   - *Article*: "Diya Vij Wants New York to Fight for Its Artists"
   - *Descriptor*: "Funding for culture"
   - *Author*: Isa Farfan
   - *Focus*: Curator and arts administrator at Creative Time. Focuses on funding, public art, and artist welfare in New York City.

4. **Innovators Lead**: **Maurizio Cattelan**
   - *Article*: "Maurizio Cattelan Is On a Mission to Shake Us From Complacency"
   - *Descriptor*: "Powerful provocateur"
   - *Author*: Naomi Rea
   - *Focus*: Conceptual artist known for viral, provocative works (*Comedian* duct-taped banana, *America* golden toilet).

## 4. Key Structural and Editorial Observations

1. **Editorial Architecture**:
   - TIME partnered with prominent art market journalists (chiefly Julia Halperin and Artnet News editors).
   - Project leads: Merrill Fabry, Julia Halperin, and Belinda Luscombe.
   - Strategic advisor: Keith Fox.

2. **Broad Ecosystem Scope**:
   - Not only artists. The list covers the full commercial and non-commercial art machinery:
     - **Market**: Sotheby's (Charles Stewart), Christie's (Bonnie Brennan), Art Basel (Noah Horowitz), mega-galleries (Larry Gagosian, David Zwirner, Sprüth Magers), art advisor firms (Amy Cappellazzo & Yuki Terase).
     - **Institutions**: The Met (Max Hollein), LACMA (Michael Govan), Dia (Jessica Morgan), M+ (Suhanya Raffel), Guggenheim (Naomi Beckwith), V&A East (Gus Casely-Hayford), Yale School of Art (Kymberly Pinder).
     - **Law and Restitution**: Matthew Bogdanos (antiquities recovery), Christopher Marinello (looted art recovery), Luke Nikas (art litigator), Olugbile Holloway (Nigerian repatriation).
     - **Patronage and Philanthropy**: Mellon Foundation (Elizabeth Alexander), Art Bridges (Anne Kraybill), Arison Arts (Sarah Arison), Komal Shah, Ronald Lauder, Bernard Arnault, Ken Griffin.
     - **Technology & Internet**: Refik Anadol (Generative AI), Stan Prokopenko (YouTube/Online education), Devon Rodriguez (TikTok), Trevor Paglen (Surveillance/AI), Hito Steyerl (Digital critique), Mazdak Sanii (Curio).

# TIME100 Art Extraction

Dataset and documentation for the inaugural TIME100 Art 2026 collection.

## Language

**Honoree**:
An individual recognized on the TIME100 Art list.
_Avoid_: Subject, person, candidate, nominee

**Entry**:
A single collection item published by TIME.
_Avoid_: Record, card, post, item

**Pair**:
An Entry that represents two Honorees together.
_Avoid_: Duo, couple, partnership

**Category**:
One of the four thematic sections defined by TIME: Leaders, Titans, Icons, Innovators.
_Avoid_: Section, group, tag, bucket

**Tagline**:
The short role descriptor assigned by TIME below the title.
_Avoid_: Subtitle, secondary title, description

**Profile Text**:
The full editorial text and quotes written about an Entry.
_Avoid_: Blurb, bio, body, story
